# KEPT v1.4.2: the Keeper counts your reps

KEPT is an Android app for Solana: swear an Oath to a daily habit, alone or with up to three friends, stake SOL in an
on-chain escrow, and prove it every day. This release runs on **Solana Devnet** with test SOL (no real money).

## Install
1. Download **KEPT.apk** on an Android phone (Android 7.0+, arm64), or `adb install -r KEPT.apk`.
2. Allow **Install unknown apps** if Android asks.

> **Have v1.3.0 or v1.1.1 from this repo installed? Uninstall it first.** This build is signed with KEPT's release key
> (certificate SHA-256 `6D:E7:39:06:51:D5:33:78:44:9D:4A:C1:41:4A:83:2B:26:17:A0:BB:50:48:C3:F0:92:13:1A:97:46:C4:66:C8`),
> the key future updates and the Solana dApp Store build use, so Android won't install it over a build signed with
> another key.

You need a Solana wallet on the phone (e.g. Phantom) set to **Devnet**, with a little Devnet SOL
([faucet.solana.com](https://faucet.solana.com)).

## What's new
- **Live rep counter, nine activities:** push-ups, squats, jumping jacks, lunges, sit-ups, high knees, burpees,
  overhead presses and a timed plank. The phone counts with an on-device pose model from **any horizontal angle**
  (front, side, diagonal, turning mid-set), then the server **recounts the whole pose trace** with the same
  deterministic engine before anything goes on chain. A "Freeze!" at a rep the server picks from the Solana blockhash
  takes a live photo (hold still, one hand up) that the AI judge checks. If a set fails, the app says which part
  (the raised hand, the pose or the recount) and the Keeper's reason. Facing the phone works with your feet out of view (tested on a real
  front-view set: 10 of 10 counted, recounted and kept). Cheats (half reps, knee push-ups, piked hips, waist-bend squats, one-arm
  jacks, frozen poses, replayed sets) are refused.
- **The Keeper hypes you:** counts aloud and throws in lines between reps in its own ElevenLabs voice. A Settings switch
  turns on the no-filter version.
- **Ask the Keeper is an agent:** hold to ask anything about your Oaths ("what do I still have to do today?", "how
  are my friends doing?"). It looks up your habits and targets, today's progress for every member, HP, time left,
  stake, miss cost, payouts and receipts, then answers in its voice in one or two short sentences. It never reads out
  addresses, hashes or codes; friends are "friend 1", "friend 2".
- **Stop the Keeper:** a "Stop the Keeper" button shows whenever the Keeper is talking (answers, verdicts,
  transaction read-outs, rep hype); one tap silences it.
- **Explain before sign:** before the wallet opens, the Keeper reads out what the transaction does and roughly how
  much SOL it moves ("about 0.07 SOL").
- **Public, verifiable receipts:** every verdict is a receipt whose SHA-256 goes on chain with the day's check-in;
  receipts are public files on Supabase Storage, and **Audit the Keeper** re-hashes them on the phone against Solana.
- **Live updates:** Supabase Realtime pushes a friend's proof to everyone in the Oath.
- **Group Oaths:** start with 2, 3 or 4 members; misses are split between that day's keepers; settlement is automatic.
- **Oath names survive a reinstall:** an Oath without a name saved on the phone is named by its habits
  ("Pushups · Squats") instead of a generated name.

## Known limits
- Keep the phone level and low (on the floor for push-ups, plank, sit-ups; upright on a chair for standing moves).
  A camera looking down from above weakens counting.
- Filmed from behind and to the side, where the feet are hidden behind the body, knees-down push-ups and planks
  can't be told apart from full ones; from the front and sides they are refused.
- High knees filmed exactly side-on can miss some lifts; front and back views count fully.
- Phantom shows "identity could not be verified" for the default app identity. Settings › "Wallet identity: KEPT's
  own site" uses KEPT's verified site instead; switch it off if your wallet won't connect.

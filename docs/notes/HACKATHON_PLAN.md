# KEPT hackathon plan (2026-11-26)

Generated by a research + 5-strategy + 3-judge workflow. Source data: scratchpad strategy-result.json.

## Positioning

KEPT: Oaths you can't fake. Up to four friends stake SOL on a daily habit and share one HP bar, so a faked day takes money from the friends who were honest. The Keeper, KEPT's half-warden, half-bookie reaper, is a real AI agent with its own Solana wallet. It judges every photo proof on the server against a challenge drawn from a recent slot hash, says its verdict out loud, and writes a receipt hash to Devnet that anyone can re-check from the app. Rivals are solo or zero-sum, where friends profit when you fail (Lock-In, Moonwalk, Stakehold, SleepFi), and none of them puts a checkable AI verdict on-chain. Pitch line: "Habit apps trust your word. Crypto habit apps trust your phone. KEPT trusts neither."

## Chosen strategy

Base: Strategy #4, "The Keeper Keeps Receipts". It had the best combined judge score (40 + 37 + 40 = 117, against 111 for #0 and 108 for #1), and two of the three judges backed it. Grafted onto it:
(a) From #0: failed and cheat verdicts also go on-chain, as an SPL Memo from the verifier wallet carrying sha256(fail receipt), so no program upgrade is needed.
(b) From #0: explain-before-sign. The backend decodes the unsigned create/join/claim transaction with the Anchor IDL coder, and the Keeper reads the exact lamports and vault PDA aloud before the MWA prompt.
(c) From #0: the Receipt sheet becomes an "Audit the Keeper" screen.
(d) From #1: real SIWS through MWA sign_in_payload, Keeper Knocks notifications that deep-link into the proof camera, and the SGT check turned into a badge instead of a wall (Tier 1).
(e) From #3: settle eligibility first (the repo origin is the friend's github.com/ekanshvcpkg/KEPT-DAPP, and docs/SUBMISSION_EVIDENCE.md shows an Oct 2026 submission).
(f) From #2: HP-priced pledges ("back, don't bet") as the business-model and roadmap slide only. Build them only as a Tier 2 stretch.

Fatal flaws the judges flagged, and how each is handled:
- #0's program upgrade inside the window: moved to Tier 2. Verifiable AI uses the record_checkin proof_hash that is already deployed.
- #0's "a cheat verdict drops HP on-chain" claim: dropped. HP is replayed from the days_kept bitmasks, so a failed day just isn't kept, and the video says exactly that.
- #4's "passes only on-chain": fixed by the Memo graft.
- #4's 120-second no-grace window: fixed with a pre-boundary cutoff, a fast model, a Helius RPC and rehearsal.
- #4's "no new on-chain work during the hackathon": answered honestly in the README. The new work is the verifier agent, the receipts and the live loop. record_verdict is a Tier 2 option if an on-chain developer is free.

### Why

1) Every track depends on the same audit fixes: a hosted backend, a server-side proof check, and an app-driven Live loop on Devnet. The plan that does those first, with the least new risk, has the highest chance of actually shipping (judges estimated about 70%, against about 40% for #0 and 25-35% for #2 and #3).
2) It fixes the fatal audit hole, where proofs could be forged with 3 HTTP calls, in a way the top-100 "code inspection + live demo verification" stage will reward (hack.dev3pack.xyz/faq). Then it turns that fix into the product: verdict receipts on-chain, which is the Agentic track's own "verifiable AI (on-chain records of model outputs)" example.
3) It needs no program change. lib.rs:232-248 already gates record_checkin on config.verifier and emits proof_hash in CheckinRecorded, and the team already controls its own program 7VpBDwXiDvvaG29VBFUPankoSWFVanyD9KcT7wbmLVzj (commit 31649d4).
4) Every sponsor integration it uses does real work: Supabase for Postgres, Storage and Realtime, and ElevenLabs for Voice Design and TTS on verdicts and transaction explainers. That matters because the FAQ says "judges check qualification requirements" and the bundled partner copy says cosmetic mentions do not qualify. Byte, Firecrawl and Pollar would all be bolted on.
5) It fits the constraints: an 11 GB laptop (the APK is built on the existing .github/workflows/release.yml, not locally), a Samsung with no SGT (allowlist), and a 27-29 Nov window.

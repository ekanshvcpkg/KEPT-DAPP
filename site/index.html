Keep your word. Stake on it.

KEPT is an Android app for Solana. You swear an Oath to do a daily habit, alone or with up to three friends, lock a stake in an on-chain escrow, and prove the habit every day with one live photo.

Test version. KEPT currently runs on Solana Devnet. Stakes use Devnet test SOL, which has no monetary value. Live mode is a closed test for wallets on our tester list; Demo mode is open to everyone and needs no wallet.

Oaths

Pick a habit, 3, 7 or 14 days, solo or group, and a stake. A Solana program holds the stakes until the Oath ends.

One HP bar

The whole group shares one health bar. A missed day costs HP and 10% of that member's stake, which goes to the members who kept.

The Keeper

An AI judge on our server checks each photo for the habit's object and a hand gesture drawn from a recent blockhash.

Receipts you can check

Each verdict becomes a receipt. Kept days put its hash on chain; failed checks are published in a memo. "Audit the Keeper" re-checks them on your phone.

Explain before sign

Before your wallet opens, KEPT decodes and simulates the transaction and shows exactly how much SOL leaves your wallet.

The Keeper speaks

Verdicts and transaction summaries are read aloud in the Keeper's voice, made with ElevenLabs. You can turn it off.

Get KEPT

KEPT is being submitted to the Solana dApp Store. Until it is listed, the Android APK and source code are on GitHub: github.com/ekanshvcpkg/KEPT-DAPP. Android 7.0 or newer, arm64, with a Mobile Wallet Adapter wallet such as Phantom.

Who makes KEPT

KEPT is built by a small independent team as a hackathon project. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Solana Mobile, the Solana Foundation, Phantom, OpenRouter, Google or ElevenLabs.

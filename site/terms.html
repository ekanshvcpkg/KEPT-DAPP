KEPT is a test app on Solana Devnet. Stakes, payouts and the test SKR token use Devnet test tokens, which have no monetary value and can't be exchanged for money. Use KEPT at your own risk.

1. What KEPT is

KEPT is a habit commitment app. You can swear an Oath to do a daily habit, alone or with others, lock a stake in an escrow run by a Solana program, and prove the habit each day with a live photo. Under the Oath's rules, a missed day moves part of the missing member's stake to the members who kept that day, minus a fee to KEPT's treasury. You can stake only on your own habit, never on someone else's result.

2. Test version, test tokens

  • KEPT currently runs on Solana Devnet, a test network. Devnet tokens have no value, and Devnet itself can be reset, slow or unavailable.
  • In this test build, a "day" may last only a few minutes so the app can be tried quickly.
  • Live mode is a closed test for wallets on our tester list. Demo mode is a simulation with a sample account and moves no real or test funds.
  • Features, rules and availability may change or stop at any time while KEPT is being tested.

4. The AI judge can be wrong

Proof photos are judged by an AI vision model (the Keeper). AI models make mistakes. The Keeper can reject a real photo or accept a wrong one. In Live mode a rejected day can't be appealed yet. A day judged as missed is settled on chain by the program's rules, and on-chain results can't be reversed by us. Receipts let anyone check which verdict the chain acted on. They don't make the verdict correct.

5. Your wallet and transactions

  • KEPT shows what each transaction does before your wallet opens. Your wallet's approval screen is final. Only approve transactions you understand.
  • Blockchain transactions can't be undone. Network fees apply even if a transaction fails.
  • Smart contracts and software can have bugs. The KEPT program and app have not had an independent security audit.
